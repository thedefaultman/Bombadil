This page defines the words Bombadil uses for its parts, its screen and its rules, in alphabetical order. A word that means two things has one row for each. Rows for what is on `main` were checked against the code, as the Verified line says. A row that ends with (in progress) describes work built on a branch that is not on `main`, and one that ends with (designed) describes a decided design with no code, so its meaning comes from the brief or page it links. A row that names a built part and a designed part says which is which. The [roadmap](roadmap.md) says where each piece stands and [decisions](decisions/README.md) says why it was chosen.
