Bombadil Tends Itself

It counts what you keep asking and offers to turn it into a word, a widget, an app or a routine. It checks itself, fixes what it can prove while you are away, and writes up what it cannot for the project. You see a chip beside the pill, a row in Away, and an Undo on everything it changed.

Accepted by Daniel on 30 Sep 2026; the build of the first two pieces has started. This sits on the four confirmed briefs and on Riding with Bombadil. The full text is design/self-improvement-brief.md beside this page; where it changes an earlier decision, the change is listed and argued near the end.

The rules

  1. Counting and checking are not work; fixing is, and it runs on his words.Counts read what the machine already writes, stay here and call no model. Probes only read. Fixing needs his yes, given once in his own words and kept as a unit he can pause and stop in Autopilot.
  2. An idea is a strip he can ignore, never a popup.A chip beside the pill while something waits. Nothing on the line, no keyboard, no sound, no mark on the dot, never during a turn. Silence is an answer, and it expires.
  3. A tap is the ask.Every build an offer leads to is an ordinary turn with its restore point, started by his tap on the row. The agent never builds from a count on its own.
  4. It fixes only what it can prove, and it cannot touch what judges it.A fix lands only when a test that failed before passes after, run by a fixed program the fixer cannot edit. What it cannot prove here, it writes up.
  5. Every change has an undo that does not go through what changed.Its own code lands as a new generation beside the old one, and a guard outside both puts the old one back if the bar or agentd stops answering.
  6. It works in the gaps.Only while he is away, one job at a time, under a written budget, never on a turn's path. His Enter parks it at the next step.
  7. What he would notice waits for him; the rest lands quietly with Undo.What he sees or types into, and what the agent is told, waits for Use it. Nothing leaves the machine until he presses Submit on a page that shows exactly what goes.

The loop in four lanes

Notice and Check only read. Mend and Tell are the only lanes that change anything, and each has a gate he or a fixed program holds.

What has it noticed, and what did it change about itself?

The chip noticed 2 right of the pill counts decisions waiting: offers, changes waiting for Use it, reports ready to send. It exists only while something waits. Hover to peek, click to keep it up, or say noticed.

The rule that picks the recommended one

  1. Retries, undos, or corrections of how Bombadil behaved: I, not offered; it becomes a finding.
  2. Corrections of style: G.
  3. Every ask opened the same existing thing: A.
  4. Waiting on an event (“is it done”, “tell me when”): F.
  5. Near one clock time on 3 days, and it changed something: E.
  6. Reading something: C if it changes within the hour, else B with its word.
  7. Adding to one set of data: D, extending the app that holds it.

Ties go to the smallest new surface and the cheapest undo: A, G, B, F, E, C, D.

A word can only open or show

A words.toml row maps his exact phrase to an app, panel, card or desk face. It never runs a shell command, sudo or the model, is matched after projects, sessions, apps and aliases, and never shadows them. At most 30; unused for 28 days, it is put away with Bring back.

When the thing already has a word, the offer only says so and makes nothing: Memory already opens with the word memory. [Got it].

Fixing itself

Counting and checking are not work, like the welcome line built locally. Fixing is, so it runs on one standing yes: his own words from 28 Sep, confirmed by one tap, kept as a unit in Autopilot. The dev brief already reads a named check as a standing instruction.

The mender, the gate and the budget

The sessionA dev-layer session, role mender, in ~/Projects/.work/Bombadil/<fp> at the exact commit the machine runs. Fenced, no os-mcp, no web. Allowed git, python, pytest, ruff, qmllint; denied sudo, ssh, push and the protected paths. Never the bypass flags agentd uses for his turns.
Its taskA template filled from the finding: evidence marked as untrusted data, the probe id, write the failing test first, one commit for the test and one for the fix, do not push. His prompts and page text never enter it.
The gateRoot-owned, shipped in the image. Runs the base commit's own tests plus only the new ones. The new test must fail before and pass after; no test removed or weakened; nothing protected touched; at most 5 files and 200 lines.
The reviewerThe other provider, read-only: diff, finding and gate output. A block turns the fix into a report. A failure goes back once; a second makes it a report.
WhenAway 10 minutes or locked, on power or above 50% battery, not metered, no turn running or queued, not within 5 minutes of his last prompt. His Enter parks it.
How muchOne mender at a time. 30 minutes and 40 turns a fix, 2 attempts a finding, 3 starts a day, 8 a week, 2 landings a day, under half the provider's windows once the meter is read. Two misses in a row pause it.

Turning it down, with no new word

hide noticed hides the widget and holds offers, the desk's own verb on a widget name. stop mending and pause mending are Autopilot's verbs on its row, and hold everything pauses both for a day. The window has Forget what I ask and Clear what it found. What landed stays, each with its Undo.

Cut, and why

  • An offer on the line, a popup or a toast. Suggestion features die from interruption.
  • A dashboard or score of habits or of its own improvements. A gauge with nothing wrong, and exactly the thing he would feel he maintains.
  • A settings screen with thresholds, or a self-improvement mode. Maintenance by another name.
  • The fixer editing its tests, probes, gate, guard or budget. Agents game what they can touch: METR saw o3 reward-hack in 39 of 128 RE-Bench runs, and the Darwin Gödel Machine faked a test log.
  • A fix with no failing test first. Nothing proves it did anything.
  • The machine pushing, opening pull requests or merging. Landing in the repository stays his word.
  • Fixing his projects or documents. It mends Bombadil and what it made, nothing of his.
  • A model counting, or embeddings for grouping. Cost and noise; the brain brief cut both.
  • Self-written skills, notes or lessons. A skills benchmark found no average benefit from self-generated skills, and they carry mistakes forward.
  • Retrying until green, parallel menders, fixing during his turn. A token furnace; one turn, one restore point.
  • Screenshots in reports, auto-filing with a stored token. Privacy, and nothing leaves without his Submit.
  • Counting coding-session prompts, for now. A session's repeated instruction is its project's check.
  • A daily or weekly digest. A second history beside Away and Time.

Risks and open questions

  • The room is unproven. Hyprland does not start nested in the cloud container, and a headless Hyprland on the laptop VM's software rendering is unchecked. Without it, focus and layout bugs in the bar are reports, not fixes.
  • The fence is not built. Sudo is passwordless by design and the repository pre-approves sudo *. The mender must wait for the dev fence and managed settings that outrank the repository's.
  • A root agent can defeat the fixed part. The machine's own agent has sudo; hooks and hash checks catch accidents and injected text, not a determined root agent.
  • Metering is moving. Headless CLI billing was announced and paused; if it changes, the loop drops to finding and reporting.
  • Grouping is unproven on real data, and one person may produce few offers. The precision check and four weeks of counted answers decide the numbers.
  • Generations and root undo can skew; a manifest's requirements make the stubs fall back to the system tree.
  • Config split. skel is copied once at install, so fixes to hyprland.lua never reach an installed machine.
  • Privacy. The ledger holds his words; they stay here, at most eight reach the fast model per offer, and reports carry counts only.
