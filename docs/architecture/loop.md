The loop is meant to let Bombadil keep itself in good shape without becoming a chore for the person. It will count the requests the person keeps typing and offer, once and on a small chip beside the pill, to turn a repeat into something smaller. It will check its own state for bugs, fix what it can prove while the person is away, and write up what it cannot for the project. It exists because Bombadil's faults are found by hand as of 2026-10-01, by someone testing in a VM and relaying what they hit, and because history that was not recorded cannot be recovered later. The design holds itself to a budget: in an ordinary week, no line above the pill (except a put-back), no mark on the dot, no card raised on its own, at most three new offers and no new word to learn.

## What it will do

- **Noticing (built on an unmerged branch).** The ledger in `turns.jsonl` will keep what a later count needs: a lasting `id`, `seconds`, `origin`, `tools`, `cost` and a row for `stop`, and `model` as the provider reports it (`main` already writes a `model` key, as the model the provider was set to), and old rows will stay readable. A typed prompt that went to the model will count as an ask; `!` lines, sign-ins, private things, pasted text over 300 characters and turns that were stopped, failed or undone within 60 s will not. Asks will be grouped with no model, from their words, a verb class, the apps and panels they name and the route the turn took. Three asks on two days within 21 days, and worth building, will become one offer: at most one new offer a day and three a week, "Not now" returning only when the count has doubled or after 30 days, and two silent expiries or two Nevers in a row resting every offer for 30 days. The bar rises from 3 to 5 asks when fewer than 3 of the last 10 offers were taken (`asks_needed` in `src/bombadil/loop/offers.py`). Counts will live in `loop.db`, read from `turns.jsonl` by byte offset 150 rows to a transaction, by a service inside agentd (`LoopService`, one background worker that owns the database) that is never on a turn's path. The service counts when agentd starts, about 2 s after the last of a burst of new rows, and at least every 10 minutes; while a client is connected it reads the prober's findings about every 20 s; and it records an offer as shown only while no turn runs, nothing is queued and the bar has said `hello` (`DEBOUNCE`, `INGEST_EVERY`, `FINDINGS_EVERY` and `_idle` in `src/bombadil/loop/service.py`). On the branch an offer can build only a word (a row in `words.toml` that opens an app or a panel and runs nothing else, at most 30 active ones) or a new app (an ordinary turn that the person's tap starts). The other forms the brief lists (a card, a widget, a routine, a watcher, a preference) are designed, and each waits for its builder. One optional model call may name or split a group about to be offered; the branch ships it switched off. The Noticed window, a kit app, lists what they ask most, what changed (each with Undo), what it found, what they said no to and the words made. Once a day the service will put away every word not used for 28 days and write an `improve` row for it, whose Undo brings the word back (`_sweep` in `src/bombadil/loop/service.py`, `put_away` and `words_unused` in `src/bombadil/loop/words.py`).
- **The chip and its card (built on an unmerged branch).** A small chip beside the pill says how many things wait, and a card above it says what they are (`shell/LoopState.qml`, `shell/NoticedChip.qml`, `shell/NoticedCard.qml`).
  - **When it shows.** The chip reads `noticed N` and sits right of the pill, past the right desk strips when they are shown, or above the pill when there is no room. It is visible only while N is more than 0, Noticed is not hidden, no turn runs and agentd is connected, and it never turns up by itself while the pill has the keyboard; one already up stays. N is the number of rows waiting, which `LoopService._state` builds: at most one offer, plus the findings not yet reported, plus the reports held for sending. It is not capped at the three rows the card shows.
  - **How the card opens and closes.** Resting the pointer on the chip for 250 ms peeks the card, which closes 300 ms after the pointer has left the chip and the card. A click keeps the card up, gives the pill the keyboard and sends `noticed_do` with op `open`, which opens the Noticed window; a second click, Esc, a turn starting, the pill giving the keyboard back or agentd going away puts a kept card away. The words `noticed`, `open noticed` and `show noticed` also keep it up, and `hide noticed` hides the chip and the card until one of those words is said.
  - **What the card shows.** It is 300 px wide and titled Noticed, with one line of why such as "1 idea · 1 thing it found", at most three rows, then "Resting offers until <day>" while offers rest, and "Lately: N changes this week" with a See all button that opens the window. A row shows the person's own words, how often, one line of what would happen, one button (`BUTTONS` in `src/bombadil/loop/service.py`: "Make the word" for form A, "Make an app" for D, "Got it" when the thing already has a word, "Send to the project" for a finding, "See the report" for a held one), a quiet "Not now" and "Never", and up to two "Other ways". Pressing the button is the ask: no second question follows.
- **Seeing itself (built on an unmerged branch).** 23 probes, pure functions over `hyprctl` JSON, the bar's rectangles, coredumps and the ledger, will look for a drawer that took no keyboard, stacked app windows, a window under the bar, a monitor under 1024 px and a Super tap that left the pill without the keyboard. An invariant will count only when it is red again 500 ms later; an event, a crash or a drift counts at its first sighting, and friction at three sightings on two days (or at one sighting with another probe firing within five minutes). A probe that goes red and then green on its retry three times in a week is left out and becomes a finding about itself (`src/bombadil/loop/findings.py`). A finding will keep an evidence bundle under `findings/<fp>/`. When the person asks, it will become a report built from typed fields with at most three log lines, opened as a prefilled new-issue page in the browser panel; the person will press Submit and no token will be stored. The bar will send `hello`, `alive`, its rectangles, `focus_ack`, `focus_cancel` and `friction`, and agentd will answer `ping`. The prober, `bombadil-probe`, is a user unit that agentd starts and that outlives it. It looks when Hyprland says a window opened or closed or a panel slid in or out (the layout checks run about a second later, the drawer's about two seconds after it opens), and once a minute while the person is away (no prompt for 10 minutes, or the screen locked); it reads coredumps at most every 5 minutes and, while the person is away, runs the idle doctor once a day (`src/bombadil/loop/runner.py`). Fixtures cover five of the six bugs found by hand; the one left, Chromium's first-run dialog, is a report by design. `main` runs agentd and the bar as user units with `Restart=always` (`bombadil-agentd.service` and `bombadil-shell.service`, see [the session stays alive](session-services.md)), which the brief names as the way restarts and failed units become signals at all; the branch predates them, because it last merged `main` at `3d9bc36`.
- **The room and the gate (designed).** A fixed program in `/usr/lib/bombadil/fixed/`, root-owned and shipped in the image, will judge every candidate fix and the fixer cannot edit it. The new test must fail on the base commit and pass on the candidate, no test may be removed, weakened or skipped, no protected path may change (the loop, probes, gate, guard, existing tests, sudoers, managed settings, the fence, snapshots, rollback, installer, CI) and a fix will be at most 5 files and 200 lines. Tiers will run from ruff and pytest through offscreen QML tests to the room: a second agentd and bar on scratch `BOMBADIL_STATE`, `BOMBADIL_RUNTIME` and `BOMBADIL_SOCKET`. The other provider will review read-only. The room is unproven: whether a headless Hyprland starts on a VM's software rendering is unchecked, and without it focus and layout bugs in the bar stay reports.
- **The mender, generations and the guard (designed).** One tap on Start mending, once, will write `bombadil-mend.timer` with the person's own words and the budget inside it, listed in Autopilot ([riding brief](../design/passenger-brief.md)) with Pause and Stop. A fenced coding session with role `mender` ([coding sessions](coding-sessions.md)) will work in a clone while the person is away, at most 3 starts and 2 landings a day. It will fix its own words and data, its apps and kit extras, and Bombadil's code that the gate can prove; compositor config, boot, `/etc`, sudoers, packages, vendor bugs and the loop itself will be reports. A passing fix will be exported as a generation under `~/.local/share/bombadil/gen/<sha>/` and switched by a `current` link, with `previous` kept. `bombadil-guard`, outside every generation, will check agentd, the bar and a few other signs for 10 minutes after a landing, and flip back on three reds in a row, a coredump of a Bombadil program or a failed unit. Undo will flip the link with no model.
- **Growth (designed; putting a word away is built on an unmerged branch).** New kit components will go only into an overlay named `BombadilExtra` and never replace `Theme` or `AppWindow`, at most 2 a week and 12 in all. Processes will be `bombadil-*` user units with an `[X-Bombadil]` record, at most 8, each an Autopilot row. On the branch a word unused for 28 days is put away once a day, and Undo or the `bring_back` op brings it back and starts the 28 days again (`_sweep` in `src/bombadil/loop/service.py`, `put_away` and `bring_back` in `src/bombadil/loop/words.py`). A widget or a unit unused for 28 days will be put away with Bring back in the same way, and a kit component unused for 60 days will be archived and stay loadable; those parts are designed. The loop will write no skills, notes or lessons for the agent to read.
- **The desk face (designed).** Noticed will be a desk widget at the top of the right rail (bottom up: Needs you, Away, Machine, Noticed) that starts as its strip. On `main` a widget can be folded to its strip by hand and the set of folded ones starts empty, so a `noticed` widget would need to start in it (the `desk.py` row below). A night's changes will merge into one Away row, "Changed 2 things about itself", Time in the [brain](brain.md) will show `kind: "improve"` rows in the machine's orange, and Watching will show a "Mending" row while a fix runs. The chip and its peek card beside the pill belong to the first piece and are built on the branch (see the chip above). On `main` the right rail holds Needs you, Away, Machine and Tips (`DeskState.widgetIds`), and Away has its feed, agentd's `away` message. The Tips card ([Tips](tips.md), built) holds the rail's top slot until Noticed becomes a rail widget.

## How it works

Counting and offering run inside agentd as `LoopService`, beside the turns and never on their path. Checking runs in its own user unit, `bombadil-probe`, so that it outlives an agentd crash and can see it. Both write `loop.db`, and the bar draws what they found as the chip and its card.

## Where state lives

The first two rows are `main`'s own files; every other file here is the branch's, and `main` has none of them. The loop's files sit in `paths.loop_dir()`, in the state directory, and `words.toml` in the config directory: both are in the home folder, which a restore point does not cover ([restore points](restore-points.md)). The loop has no socket of its own: its messages travel over agentd's.

| Path | Holds | Written by |
|---|---|---|
| `paths.state_dir()/turns.jsonl` | the ledger, and the `improve` rows of the trail | agentd only (`_log_line`); the service hands its own rows to that writer |
| `paths.state_dir()/turns/<ms>-<id>.jsonl` | a turn's events, read for the route of a turn | agentd |
| `paths.loop_dir()/loop.db` with `-wal` and `-shm` | counts, groups, offers, what the person said no to, findings, the byte offset read so far in `turns.jsonl`, and whether Noticed is hidden | the service in agentd and the prober, both writers of one WAL file |
| `signals.jsonl`, `bar.json`, `agentd.json` in the loop directory | what the bar and agentd say about themselves | agentd |
| `doctor.json` | the last idle doctor | the prober |
| `findings/<fp>/` | the evidence bundle of a finding | the prober |
| `reports/<fp>.md` | the report held for sending | the service, when the person asks for the report |
| `trash/<name>-<time>` | the folder of an app made for an offer, after its Undo | the service |
| `config.toml` | optional numbers (`[offers]`, `[refine]`) | the person |
| `paths.words_file()` (`words.toml`, in the config directory) | the words made, at most 30 | the service; the launcher reads it |
| `bombadil-probe.service` | the prober's user unit: `Restart=always`, `RestartSec=5`, `Nice=15`, idle CPU and I/O scheduling, no `[Install]` section | started by agentd with `systemctl --user start` |

The loop directory is made 0700, and `loop.db`, its `-wal` and `-shm` and `reports/` are kept owner-only (files 0600, directories 0700, older ones tightened) because they hold the person's own words: `src/bombadil/loop/db.py` does this for the directory and the database files (`connect`, `private_dir`), and `src/bombadil/loop/report.py` for `reports/` and the 0600 report file (`hold`). A damaged `loop.db` is never replaced: the service says so once on stderr and counts nothing until the person deletes it with its `-wal` and `-shm`, after which `turns.jsonl` is counted again from the top and what was said no to, what was offered and what was forgotten are lost.

## Principles it keeps

- [Nothing runs on its own that the person cannot see and stop](../principles.md#nothing-runs-unseen): fixing will run on one standing yes, kept as a unit the person can pause and stop, and an offer will never start anything. The trap is a hidden timer, or letting a count or a probe start work, or taking a tap from any client: the branch accepts `noticed_do` from every client, where `main`'s agentd tells a process inside an agent's turn from the person for a press, a notice chip and a typed prompt.
- [Records answer before models](../principles.md#records-before-models): counts will read what the machine already writes, and no model counts. A fixed program decides whether a fix may land; the other provider's read-only review can only block it. The trap is asking a model to group asks; the one optional call may only name or split a group, never add a member.
- [Only the person's own press reaches other people](../principles.md#the-persons-press-reaches-people): nothing will leave until the person presses Send to the project. That opens the project's new-issue page with the report in its address, so the report reaches GitHub when the page loads, and runs one unauthenticated search of the project's public issues for the report's fingerprint (`report.already_reported`, called from `LoopService._send_report`). Nothing is posted until the person presses Submit on that page, and no token is stored. The trap is a stored token, an auto-filed issue or the person's words in a report.
- [Quiet at rest](../principles.md#quiet-at-rest): a chip while something waits, never a popup, a sound, a mark on the dot or a digest. The one line the loop may ever put above the pill is a guard's put-back. The trap is letting the chip's number grow into a mark on the dot, or putting an offer on the line, which `notices.py` makes one call away.
- [Recovery never goes through the part that broke](../principles.md#recovery-without-the-broken-part): the guard and a first line in the Lifeboat ([ux brief](../design/ux-brief.md)) will sit outside every generation, and the fixer cannot edit its judges. The trap is an undo that runs from the tree a bad fix replaced.
- [Every piece degrades](../principles.md#degrade-and-recover): counting and probes will never be on a turn's path, and either can die while turns carry on. The trap is a turn, an undo or the pill waiting on `loop.db`.

## Extending it

None of this is on `main`: the names are the unmerged branch's, and the steps come from its own notes, checked against its code.

**A new thing a repeat can become (a form)**

1. Add a `Form` to `FORMS` in `src/bombadil/loop/forms.py`: the plain sentence the card shows, and in `needs` the name of the piece that builds it. A form whose `needs` is not in `BUILT` is never offered, and `forms.available` names the piece it waits for.
2. Add that name to `BUILT` once the builder exists. At the branch's head it holds `words` and `create_app`.
3. Teach `LoopService` to run it: a button in `BUTTONS` and a branch in `_accept`, both in `src/bombadil/loop/service.py`.
4. Write the change into the trail with `_improve` and an `undo` that names an op, and add that op to `_op_undo` and to `UNDOABLE`, so the Noticed window can offer Undo for it.
5. Cover it in `tests/test_loop_forms.py` and `tests/test_loop_service.py`.

**A new check**

1. Write a function over the `Observation` in `src/bombadil/loop/probes.py` and register it with `@probe(id, component, kind, title, what, needs)`. It returns `red()`, `green()` or `unchecked()`, and it only reads: no socket, no process, no file, and no window title, prompt or path in what it reports.
2. If it needs data no `Observation` field holds, add the field, a collector of the same name to `Collectors` in `src/bombadil/loop/runner.py` and the field to `COLLECTED`. A collector never raises and never waits past its own deadline.
3. Add a bad and a fixed fixture under `tests/fixtures/loop/probes/`, red on the bug and green on the fix, and a test in `tests/test_loop_probes.py`.

**A new surface (another shell, or a command)**

Speak the messages in the interface table: `noticed_state`, `noticed_list` and `noticed_do` from the client, `noticed` and `noticed_full` back. `_noticed_do` in `bin/bombadil`, which `bombadil loop forget` uses to send the op `forget_asks`, is the smallest client.

## Known gaps

Each is read from the branch's code at `05a5ebf` unless it says `main`.

- No fix is made by the loop. `Finding.fixable` is `False` for every finding (`src/bombadil/loop/findings.py`), and `bombadil check`, `bombadil-room` and `bombadil-guard` appear nowhere in the branch's `bin/`, `src/`, `iso/`, `scripts/`, `shell/` or `share/`.
- An offer can build only a word or a new app: `BUILT` in `src/bombadil/loop/forms.py` holds `words` and `create_app`, the card, widget, routine, watcher and preference forms wait for their builders, and the kit-part and fix forms are `offered=False`.
- `LoopService.on_message` takes `noticed_do` from any client, where `AgentD._persons` on `main` refuses a process inside an agent's turn for a notice chip, a dismissal and the press on Send.
- Neither `rest.json` nor `requeued` is read anywhere in `src/bombadil/loop/`: `not_ours` in `src/bombadil/loop/probes.py` tells a limit from the provider's words and the row's `rate_limit`.
- The optional model call in `src/bombadil/loop/refine.py` is off unless the loop's `config.toml` turns it on, and it has not been checked on both providers.
- A machine with no user systemd has no prober: `start_prober` in `src/bombadil/loop/service.py` runs `systemctl --user start`, and `bombadil loop status` then reports the prober as not running or not known.
- A damaged `loop.db` stops all counting until the person deletes it, and deleting it loses what was said no to (`src/bombadil/loop/db.py`).
- The branch cannot be merged without hand work; the files are named under "Expect hand merging" below.

## Before you build on it

- **Order (the brief).** 1 noticing (M, first because history cannot be recovered), 2 seeing itself (M to L), then 3 the room and the gate (starting with a spike: does a second headless Hyprland run in a VM), 4 the mender, generations and the guard, 5 growth, 6 the desk face. Pieces 1 and 2 come first and follow the app kit. The mender needs the coding sessions' fence, out-of-sight sessions, a conversation id that outlives a reboot and a login, a build stamp, a clone, pytest and ruff in the image, and the Lifeboat line. `main` saves `AgentD.session_id` in `agentd-state.json` under `paths.runtime_dir()`, so the conversation survives a restart of agentd (systemd brings the daemon back), but not a reboot or a new login. Its quota rule (start below half of each window) needs the rate-limit event in the ledger row: `main` parses it (`Claude._events`) and keeps it only for the limit decision, and the branch writes it as `rate_limit`.
- **Waits on the owner.** Every default is confirmed (options 1a, 2a and 3a chosen and the plan approved on 30 Sep 2026). Open for the owner, later: whether other people's Bombadils should ever report to the owner's repository, and whether anyone but the owner should default to find-and-report only. The thresholds stay guesses until four weeks of real offers are counted. The branch hard-codes the project's own repository as the default target of the issue link (`report.issue_url`), so a fork changes that before it ships reports.
- **Waits on checks.** Replay a real `turns.jsonl` and hand-label 100 pairs: grouping must reach 90% precision on "same request". The branch's numbers come from a synthetic corpus and no real result is recorded. The brief also lists a headless Hyprland in a VM, managed settings that outrank `.claude/settings.json` (which allows `Bash(sudo *)`, line 27), coredump storage on the installed image and the fast model answering a fixed JSON shape on both providers. The branch's notes list what no test of theirs reached: a real Hyprland for the chip and card, the prober unit starting under a user manager, and Chromium taking the issue page.
- **Expect hand merging.** The branch last merged `main` at `3d9bc36`, and `main` has moved on since. A trial merge into `7176e55` (`git merge-tree` with `3d9bc36` as the base, nothing written) reports conflicts in nine source files (`src/bombadil/agentd.py` with 12 hunks, `providers.py` with 3, because both sides add a `meta` event, `launcher.py`, `mcp_server.py`, `bin/bombadil`, `scripts/build-iso.sh`, `shell/shell.qml`, `shell/PillState.qml`, `shell/StatusLine.qml`) and in `README.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` and five test files (`tests/desktop/driver.py`, `test_iso_profile.py`, `test_launcher.py`, `test_providers.py` and `test_watch.py`). `paths.py`, `watch.py` and `bombadil-smoke` merge without a conflict although both sides add code there. Merged text still needs care. Both sides write a `model` key into a model row. The branch starts its app turn with `agentd.handle(prompt, None)` (no client), and on `main` `handle` asks `_persons(writer)` first, where `_peer_pid(None)` raises `AttributeError`, so the app form would fail. The branch also answers any `access` but `ready` with "needs you to sign in", which the `resting` access on `main` makes untrue.
- **Meets the resting state.** While the AI rests (`rest.json`), counting and probes need no model and go on. A turn the limit stopped (`requeued: true`) is not a failed ask and not a finding, the optional naming call and an offer that starts a turn should wait for the limit, and the design of the poor man switch says a rest is never a finding ([the resting state](poor-man-switch.md), [its brief](../design/poor-man-switch-brief.md)). The branch reads neither `rest.json` nor `requeued`.
- **Do not break.** The fields already in a `turns.jsonl` row and the `kind: "local"` rows, which `bombadil history` prints as `HH:MM  prompt: result` and the VM smoke test matches (`launcher_done`). The exactness of `launcher.match`: a word may only open or show and never shadows an app, a panel or a command. Bare `undo` meaning the machine's last turn. `bombadil-rollback`, which stays plain bash. The smoke checks named above, which the probes are measured against.
